# COMMUNICATION_POLICY.md

## Default Rule

The agent may draft; the human approves and sends.

## Allowed Agent Support

- summarize communications
- classify inbox items
- draft agendas
- draft follow-up emails
- draft status updates

## Human Approval Required

- external email sending
- collaborator commitments
- grant or institutional messaging
- sensitive personnel communication

## Notes

- Keep communication rules explicit and reviewable.
