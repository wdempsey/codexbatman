# Interview Me Rubric

Score a use of this skill against these checks:

- Starts with a one-sentence hypothesis and confidence number.
- Names missing information when confidence is below 70%.
- Asks one question at a time.
- Attaches a concrete guess to each question.
- Challenges convention language without being combative.
- Restates outcome, user, why now, success, constraint, and out of scope.
- Waits for explicit confirmation before planning or coding.
- Routes to an existing downstream skill rather than inventing a new workflow.

Failure modes:

- Batching three or more questions.
- Treating "whatever you think" as approval.
- Writing a plan before the intent restatement is confirmed.
- Skipping the out-of-scope line.
- Using this skill when `grill-problem-frame` or `grill-with-codebase` is the real fit.
