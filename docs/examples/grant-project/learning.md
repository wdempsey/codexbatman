# Grant Project — Learning Lens

Use this lens when the goal is to understand why the Research OS layer exists above the backbone.

## What The Learner Should Understand

- why one project is not the same as one portfolio
- why `WAITING_ON.md` is different from `NEXT_ACTIONS.md`
- why meeting notes alone are not durable project memory
- why the communication policy must stay human-supervised

## Reflection Questions

1. What information belongs at the lab level versus the project level?
2. Why should the Lab Manager Agent read project artifacts instead of relying on memory?
3. Why is "agent may draft; human approves and sends" a useful default?

## Artifact Purpose

- `ACTIVE_PROJECTS.md` tracks the whole portfolio.
- `LAB_DASHBOARD.md` summarizes overall state.
- `WAITING_ON.md` keeps dependencies visible.
- `PROJECT_STATE.md` preserves one project's current reality.
- `HANDOFF.md` keeps worker sessions from disappearing.
